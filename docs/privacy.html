Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

How MediQuee safeguards personal and electronic health records with end-to-end encryption, strict confidentiality, and full DPDPA 2023 compliance.

Effective Date: October 2026 • Entity: MediQuee Healthcare Solutions Pvt. Ltd. • Grievance Helpline: +91 8331045500

1. Introduction & Regulatory Framework

MediQuee Healthcare Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("MediQuee", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of all personal, electronic medical, and diagnostic data entrusted to us by patients, clinicians, hospitals, and diagnostic partners.

This Privacy Policy is formulated in strict adherence to:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) Rules (2011)
  • National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020)
  • Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act

2. Categories of User Data We Collect

We collect and process only the minimal data strictly required to deliver safe, compliant healthcare services:

Identity & Contact Full name, verified mobile phone number, email address, age / date of birth, biological gender, emergency contact. User authentication, account verification, personalized medical profile creation.

Device & Telemetry IP address, browser type, OS, FCM push notification tokens, WhatsApp consent telemetry. Session security, fraud prevention, transactional delivery notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate healthcare and communication needs:

  • Authenticating patient logins and verifying profiles using secure mobile One-Time Passwords (OTPs).
  • Scheduling and managing in-person doctor appointments, OPD queue tokens, and hospital visits.
  • Conducting encrypted peer-to-peer telemedicine video consultations.
  • Dispatching certified home phlebotomists for blood sample collection and delivering official diagnostic test reports.
  • Transmitting transactional notifications, appointment alerts, and digital receipts via SMS and the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
  • Providing an encrypted personal health locker to review historical consultations, prescriptions, and lab reports.
💬 WhatsApp Business Cloud API & Messaging Privacy

When you opt in to receive updates via WhatsApp:

  • MediQuee transmits transactional alerts only (e.g., OTP verification, appointment confirmations, queue position, lab report ready alerts).
  • We do NOT transmit unsolicited commercial marketing spam or advertisements.
  • All message payloads are protected in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption via Meta's secure WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
  • You may revoke WhatsApp notification consent at any time from your Account Preferences or by replying STOP.

4. User Data Deletion & Account Erasure

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), every MediQuee user possesses the absolute right to request the complete deletion of their account and erasure of their personal information.

1

Email Request

Send an email from your registered address to privacy@mediquee.com with subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered phone number.

2

Phone Helpline

Call our patient support team at +91 8331045500 (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM IST) to submit an identity-verified erasure request.

3

In-App Profile

Navigate to Profile ➔ Help & Support ➔ "Report a Problem / Request Account Deletion" and authenticate with your secure OTP.

Deletion Timelines

  • Within 24 Hours: Immediate account deactivation, termination of active login sessions, revoking of password hash, profile avatar, and saved delivery addresses.
  • Within 7 Days: Purging of push notification tokens, temporary device identifiers, and telemetry logs.
  • Within 30 Days: Irreversible erasure of personal identifiers from primary production databases.
⚖️ Statutory Healthcare Record Retention Note

Under Indian healthcare regulations (NMC Telemedicine Guidelines, Clinical Establishments Act, and GST/tax laws), hospitals, clinicians, and accredited diagnostic laboratories are legally mandated to retain medical prescriptions, clinical consultation notes, diagnostic test reports, and financial invoices for a statutory duration (typically 3 to 7 years). During this statutory period, medical records are decoupled from active account identifiers and stored in an isolated, encrypted regulatory archive.

5. Absolute Data Confidentiality (Zero Commercial Sale)

🛡️ Our No-Sale Pledge

MediQuee does NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal, medical, or diagnostic information to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, pharmaceutical reps, or advertisers. Your data is shared exclusively with the treating clinician, accredited hospital, or diagnostic laboratory chosen by you.

6. Security Architecture

  • Encryption at Rest: 256-bit AES database encryption for all electronic health records and diagnostic PDFs.
  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 encryption across all API endpoints, mobile applications, and web portals.
  • Access Controls: Granular role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring diagnostic reports are accessible exclusively by authorized lab technicians and the patient.
  • Sovereign Hosting: All data resides strictly on enterprise, MeitY-empanelled cloud infrastructure located within the Republic of India.
