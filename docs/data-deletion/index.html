User Data Deletion Instructions

Clear, transparent, step-by-step guidance on how to request the deletion of your MediQuee account, personal profile, and associated platform data.

Entity: MediQuee Healthcare Solutions Pvt. Ltd. • Privacy Contact: privacy@mediquee.com • Helpline: +91 8331045500

Right to Data Erasure

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India and Meta Platform Terms for WhatsApp Business Cloud API integration, every user registered on MediQuee has the fundamental right to request complete erasure of their account and associated personal data.

How to Submit a Data Deletion Request

You can initiate an account and data deletion request through any of the following 3 verified methods:

1

Method 1: Email Request

Send an email from your registered email address to privacy@mediquee.com or support@mediquee.com with:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Body: Registered mobile phone number and full name
2

Method 2: Telephone Helpline

Call our patient support desk directly at +91 8331045500 (Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST). A customer support representative will initiate an identity-verified deletion workflow.

3

Method 3: Inside the MediQuee App

Log in to your MediQuee patient app, open Profile ➔ Help & Support ➔ Report a Problem / Request Account Deletion and confirm via the OTP sent to your registered phone.

Identity Verification Prior to Deletion

To protect patients from unauthorized, fraudulent, or malicious deletion attempts, an automated SMS/WhatsApp One-Time Password (OTP) verification code is sent to your registered mobile phone number. Once verified, the deletion request is immediately placed into processing.

Scope of Data Deletion & Processing Stages

Timeline Action Taken Details
Within 24 Hours Immediate Deactivation Account is disabled; active login sessions and auth tokens revoked; profile photo, password hash, saved home addresses, and preferences deleted.
Within 7 Days Telemetry & Token Purge Push notification tokens (FCM), temporary device identifiers, and telemetry logs permanently erased.
Within 30 Days Permanent Database Erasure Irreversible deletion of all personal user records and identifiers across all active production databases.
⚖️ Statutory Healthcare Archival Exception

Under the National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, Clinical Establishments Act, and Indian taxation laws, licensed medical practitioners, hospitals, and diagnostic laboratories are mandated to maintain patient consultation summaries, clinical prescriptions, diagnostic test reports, and financial invoices for a statutory retention window (typically 3 to 7 years).

During this statutory retention period, medical documents are unlinked from active user accounts and preserved in a secure, encrypted, cold-storage regulatory archive. When the statutory retention period expires, these records are permanently and irreversibly destroyed.
