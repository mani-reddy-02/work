## 2. Categories of User Data We Collect
We collect only the data necessary to provide safe and efficient healthcare coordination:

1. **Identity & Contact Details:** Full name, primary verified phone number, email address, age/date of birth, biological gender, profile picture, emergency contact.
2. **Address & Geolocation Data:** Saved delivery and home collection addresses (pincode, street, city). Real-time GPS location is requested solely with explicit user permission for home phlebotomist navigation and emergency ambulance routing.
3. **Family Members / Dependents:** Names, ages, relationships, and basic medical details for dependents added to your family health account.
4. **Clinical & Consultation Information:** Requested medical specialties, chief symptoms, appointment booking history (in-hospital OP and encrypted video consultations), digital doctor prescriptions, diagnosis notes, and medication regimens.
5. **Laboratory, Diagnostic & Pathology Data:** Diagnostic test bookings, biological sample collection logs, phlebotomy records, vital signs, reference ranges, and certified diagnostic test reports (PDFs) issued by accredited labs.
6. **Payment & Billing Metadata:** Transaction IDs, invoice numbers, amounts, and payment status. *Note: MediQuee does NOT store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. All payments are securely processed through RBI-regulated, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways.*
7. **Device & Telemetry Logs:** IP address, operating system, browser type, app access timestamps, push notification tokens, and WhatsApp opt-in telemetry.

## 5. User Data Deletion & Account Erasure (In Detail)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), every MediQuee user possesses the absolute right to request the complete deletion of their account and erasure of their personal information.

### How to Request Data Deletion:
1. **Via Email:** Send an email from your registered email address to **[privacy@mediquee.com](mailto:privacy@mediquee.com)** or **[support@mediquee.com](mailto:support@mediquee.com)** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"* and your registered mobile phone number.
2. **Via Helpline:** Call our patient support desk at **+91 8331045500** (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM IST).
3. **In-App:** Submit an account deletion request through your Profile settings.

### Verification:
To prevent fraudulent or malicious requests, an OTP verification code is sent to your registered phone number prior to processing the deletion.

### Deletion Process & Timelines:
- **Immediate (< 24 Hours):** Account is permanently deactivated; login credentials, password hash, profile avatar, saved addresses, and active sessions are revoked.
- **Within 7 Days:** Device push notification tokens and network telemetry logs are permanently purged.
- **Within 30 Days:** Complete, irreversible removal of all personal profile identifiers from active production databases.

### Statutory Medical & Legal Retention Exceptions:
Under Indian healthcare regulations (NMC Telemedicine Guidelines, Clinical Establishments Act, and GST/Tax regulations), registered hospitals, doctors, and diagnostic laboratories are legally required to archive clinical consultation records, medical prescriptions, diagnostic test reports, and financial tax receipts for a mandatory statutory period (typically 3 to 7 years).

During this period, these records are moved to an encrypted, cold regulatory archive inaccessible for regular operations, and are permanently destroyed or irreversibly anonymized once the statutory period concludes.
