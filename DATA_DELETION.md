## Right to Data Erasure
In compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)** and Meta Platform Terms for WhatsApp Business integration, users of the MediQuee platform have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions to Request Data Deletion

### Option 1: Request Deletion via Email
1. From the email address registered with your MediQuee account, send an email to:
   - **Email:** `privacy@mediquee.com` (or `support@mediquee.com`)
   - **Subject:** `Account Deletion Request`
2. Include your registered mobile phone number in the body of the email.
3. Our privacy team will respond with a verification code (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number to confirm your identity.
4. Upon OTP confirmation, your deletion request will be processed immediately.

### Option 2: Request Deletion via Phone Support
1. Call our patient support desk at **+91 8331045500** (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST).
2. Request account deletion with the support representative.
3. Verify your identity using the SMS OTP sent to your registered mobile device.
4. Your account will be queued for immediate deactivation and permanent erasure.

### Option 3: Request Deletion In-App
1. Open the MediQuee app and navigate to **Profile** -> **Help & Support**.
2. Select **Report a Problem / Request Account Deletion**.
3. Confirm with your account password/OTP.

---

## What Data Gets Deleted
Upon receipt of a verified deletion request:
- **Within 24 Hours:** Your account is immediately deactivated and disconnected from active sessions. Your password hash, profile avatar, saved home/work addresses, and contact preferences are purged.
- **Within 7 Days:** Push notification tokens, temporary device identifiers, and telemetry logs are deleted.
- **Within 30 Days:** Complete and irreversible erasure of all personal identity records from production databases.

---

## Mandatory Medical & Statutory Retention
Under the National Medical Commission (NMC) Telemedicine Practice Guidelines and Indian taxation laws, licensed medical professionals, hospitals, and diagnostic laboratories are mandated to preserve clinical consultation summaries, official medical prescriptions, diagnostic test reports, and financial tax receipts for a minimum statutory period (typically 3 to 7 years).

These clinical records are decoupled from your personal profile and archived in an encrypted, cold regulatory vault inaccessible to operational systems. Once the legal retention period elapses, archived records are permanently destroyed.
